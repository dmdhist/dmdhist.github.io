Article 1:  Name

1.  The name of the organization shall be the Hoosier Youth Philharmonic Parent Association.

2.  This organization shall be known as the HYPPA.

3.  The Hoosier Youth Philharmonic (HYP) is the combined school orchestras from Bloomington High School North (BHSN) and Bloomington High School South (BHSS) in the Monroe County Community School Corporation (MCCSC) in Bloomington, IN. 

4.  Students in the HYP attend one of the schools, and take "Orchestra" or "Advanced Orchestra" as a class at school, with associated required performances and events.

5.  In addition to required performances, HYP students also participate in non-required extracurricular activities throughout the year, including smaller ensemble concerts, fundraising events, music competitions, social activities, trips, and other types of events.

 

Article 2:  Purpose

1.  To provide financial assistance and services for the orchestra program.

2.  To plan and carry out fundraising for support of HYP activities and projects.

3.  To coordinate labor and materials needed to support HYP activities.

4.  To publicize school orchestra activities through the media that includes, but is not limited to, traditional media, a website and social media.

5.  To document orchestra activities through recordings, videos, and photographs

6.  To actively support all performances of the school orchestra programs.

7.  To provide and maintain the outfits worn by the HYP orchestra students for performances.

8.  To keep parents of orchestra members informed as to the activities and projects of the orchestra.

9.  To assume responsibility for publicity covering any activity or project sponsored by the organization.

 

Article 3:  Structure

Under the provisions specified in the bylaws, this organization shall be governed by the elected officers, the executive committee, and the membership, with the cooperation of the Music Director and Assistant Director.

 

BYLAWS

 

Article 1:  Membership

1.  The HYPPA shall have only one class of members. 

    a.  All parents/guardians of a student in the BHSN or BHSS orchestras are automatically members of the HYPPA.

    b.  In some cases, a person who is not a parent/guardian may chair an activity (as below); in that case, that person is also a member of the HYPPA.

2.  Each member shall have one vote on any matter under consideration by the membership at a meeting.

3.  The Music Director and Assistant Director of the orchestras shall be ex officio, nonvoting members of the organization.

4.  There are no membership fees.

 

Article 2:  Officers

1.  The officers of this organization shall be President, Secretary, and Treasurer.

2.  An officer must be the parent or guardian of a student who is currently enrolled in the orchestra class. President and Treasurer may not both be family members of the same student(s) in the orchestra.

3.  President:

            a.  the President will be appointed by the Music Director.

            b.  the President will serve a two-year term beginning on July 1, and can be reappointed to subsequent terms of one or two years.

4.  Secretary:

            a.  the Secretary will be appointed jointly by the President and the Music Director.

            b.  the Secretary will serve a will serve a one-year term beginning on July 1, and can be reappointed to subsequent terms of one or two years.

5.  Treasurer:

            a.  the treasurer will be appointed jointly by the President and the Music Director.

            b.  the treasurer will serve a two-year term beginning on July 1, and can be reappointed to subsequent terms of one or two years.

6.  An executive committee consisting of the President, Secretary, Treasurer, Music Director, and Assistant Director shall:

            a.  identify each summer the titles of booster chairs for the next year, and suggest members to serve as those chairs.

            b.  approve a draft annual budget for HYPPA, to be approved by vote at the first HYPPA meeting of the year.

            c.  assist the Director and Assistant Director with planning of any multi-day trips taken by the HYP.

7.  The President shall appoint all Booster Chairs for one-year renewable terms.

 

Duties of the President

1.  The President shall be in communication with the Music Director and Assistant Director and all other officers of the HYPPA.

2.  The President shall have supervision over all the business affairs of the HYPPA.

3.  The President shall be chair of the executive committee.

4.  The President shall communicate with and assist booster chairs for each event.

5.  The President shall manage an email distribution list for HYP parents, and shall create and distribute communications about HYP events.

6.  The President shall consult with the Music Director and Assistant Director about the calendar for the upcoming year, regarding events that are related to HYPPA (meetings, fundraisers, uniform checks, etc.).

7.  The President shall attend all HYP events and activities, and help in whatever way is needed.

8.  The President shall announce, set the agenda for, and preside at all HYPPA meetings and maintain order.

9.  The President shall enforce observance of the bylaws of the HYPPA.

10.  The President shall put all questions, when seconded, to a vote.

11.  The President shall call all regularly-scheduled and special meetings of the HYPPA.

12.  The President shall maintain electronic files of instructions for all booster chair positions and/or events, as well as minutes from the HYPPA meetings.

13.  The President shall be a signatory on all bank accounts belonging to the HYPPA.

14.  The President shall serve as a spokesperson for the HYPPA to the public, or at any meetings at which (s)he is called upon to attend.

15.  The President shall be available for advice and counsel during the year following the completion of his/her term of office, at which time (s)he shall have a consultation session with the incoming President and turn over all appropriate files, accounts, and passwords.

16.  The President shall maintain all records of the HYPPA, and shall own and operate an email account, email distribution list, file storage account, and any other accounts that are required for recordkeeping and communication.

 

Duties of the Secretary

1.  The Secretary is responsible for taking minutes of all HYPPA Meetings, of submitting those minutes for approval at the next meeting, and of sending them to the President for archiving.

2.  The Secretary shall be a member of the executive committee.

 

Duties of the Treasurer

1.  The Treasurer is responsible for creating and presenting a budget to the executive committee before the start of the academic year, and of presenting a budget to the membership for approval at the first meeting of the academic year.

2.  The Treasurer shall file or delegate to be filed appropriate tax forms as necessary to preserve the tax-exempt status of the association.

3.  The Treasurer shall receive all funds paid to the association, issue appropriate receipts, be solely responsible for deposit of funds in a designated depository determined by the executive committee, and shall pay all bills.  Checks may be signed by the President or Treasurer.

4.  The Treasurer shall make payments or reimbursements as needed by the Music Director, Assistant director, booster chairs, and president.

4.  The Treasurer shall be the custodian of all monies of the association, keeping records of income and expenditures in an appropriate electronic form, as well as maintaining records of the bank accounts.

5.  The Treasurer shall give a complete financial report at each meeting, including balances of the association's accounts, itemized income, itemized expenses, and ending balance.

6.  The Treasurer shall prepare a summary of finances at the end of the school year, which shall be available to all HYPPA members.  Included in the report shall be an itemization of expenses and profits from each fundraising activity.

 

Vacancies

1.  In case of a vacancy in the office of President, Secretary, or Treasurer, the Music Director will appoint a replacement.

2.  Officers may only be removed by a two-thirds vote of the membership at an association meeting.

 

 

Article III:  Meetings

1.  Meetings of the HYPPA are held once each month, during the school year, in the evening.  In the fall semester, they are held at BHSN, and in the spring semester, they are held at BHSS.  Meetings are open to any member who wants to attend.

            1.  HYPPA meetings must be advertised a week ahead of time by the President.

            2.  If a matter comes up for a vote at a meeting, each member present will have one vote.

            3.  Students who attend the meetings will not have a vote on substantive matters.

2.  Scheduled meetings may be changed by the President.

3.  Special meetings may be called by the President at any time.

4.  Members present constitute a quorum for purposes of a vote at a meeting.

5.  A simple majority of the members present and voting shall be sufficient to carry a motion.

 

Article IV:  Booster Chairs

General Booster Chair guidelines

1.  Booster Chairs are appointed by the President to coordinate and manage many aspects of the HYPPA's activities. 

2.  Booster Chairs serve one-year renewable terms, and must be filled by members. 

3.  In many cases, a Booster Chair position might be filled by multiple co-Chairs.

4.   Booster Chairs are expected to attend HYPPA meetings, at least in the months leading up to the event in question.

5.  Booster chairs shall submit to the Treasurer receipts for expenses associated with their events.  If a bill is more than $20, it must be approved by the president.

6.  In the case of expenses over $50, the Treasurer may make the debit card associated with the association's bank account available to a Booster Chair.

7.  Chairs engaging in fundraising projects or entering into contracts must have approval of the executive committee before the association's name may be used for financial obligations incurred.

8.  Chairs who establish accounts of any kind (social media, membership, etc.) that use the name of the HYP or the HYPPA must provide the President with the account username and password.

 

Standing Booster Chairs

The following Booster Chair positions are required each year, and have tasks that run throughout the year.  These committee chairs shall include:

            1.  Outfits:  order, measure, fit, clean, and store the orchestra outfits.

            2.  Webmaster:  maintain the HYP website.

            3.  Communications:  manage social media accounts, and coordinate publicity with media outlets.

            4.  Programs:  in consultation with the Director and Assistant Director, produce programs for all concerts.

            5.  Recordings:  record all concerts, and make recordings available to the Director and Assistant Director and the parents.

            6.  Booster supply room:  keep track of what is in the supply room, and furnish information to coordinators of events and activities.

            7.  Photographer:  take and/or coordinate photographs of all HYP events.

            8.  Boutique:  stock items of interest to orchestra members, and sell them at all events.

            9.  Secretary:  take minutes at all HYPPA meetings, and submit them to the president for record-keeping.

 

Special event Booster Chairs

The following Booster Chair positions will vary as events and needs change; some are in charge of a one-time event, and some oversee year-long fundraising

            1.  Year-long fundraising, which may include, but are not limited to, Grocery Cards, Scrip Cards, Restaurant Nights, and Car Washes.

            2.  Single-event Chairs, to coordinate large-scale events, often with food donations or volunteers, which may include, but are not limited to, Welcome Dessert, Night of Dance, Holiday Cider, String Spectacular reception, Chili Supper, and Awards Banquet.

            3.  Single-task Chairs, such as Directory.

 

Article V:  Finances

1.  The HYPPA is tax exempt and shall make every effort to maintain its status as a tax-exempt organization.

2.  The Treasurer and all persons of the HYPPA handling its monies shall be bonded.

3.  Monies or funds raised by, for, or in the name of the HYP under the sponsorship of the HYPPA become the property of the HYPPA.

4.  Such funds are to be used only to support approved projects, activities, and programs of the HYP.  Any expenditure not related to an existing event or booster activity that is over $400 must be approved by majority vote at a HYPPA booster meeting.

5.  The fiscal year shall be from July 1 to June 30.

 

Article VI:  Dissolution

1.  Upon dissolution or disbandment of this association, any and all unallocated cash funds shall be divided equally and turned over to Bloomington High Schools North and South for exclusive use in the orchestra program.

 

Article VII:  Standing Rules

1.  All matters pertaining to the activities, events and projects of the HYP and the HYPPA must meet with the approval of the Music Director and Assistant Director and must be in keeping with the policy of the MCCSC.

2.  The HYPPA shall maintain a post office box for the purpose of having an address and receiving mail.  The keys to this box shall be retained by the President and the Treasurer.

3.  This constitution may be amended at any regular meeting of the organization by a 2/3 vote, provided that the amendment(s) have been distributed to the membership at least 30 days prior to the meeting.
