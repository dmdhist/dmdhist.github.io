The so-called "mausoleum of Galla Placidia"

This small structure was built originally as a chapel attached to the church of the Holy Cross (which is now a ruin).  The church was founded by the empress Galla Placidia c. 425-50, and her association with it led, in the thirteenth century, to the idea that the chapel had originally contained her burial.  In fact, Galla Placidia died and was probably buried in Rome, in 450.
