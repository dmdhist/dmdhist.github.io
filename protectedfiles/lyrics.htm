Guillaume de Machaut, c. 1300-1377, Comment q'ua moy>

Comment q'ua moy lonteinne              Although you are far from me,

Soies, dame d'onnour                            my noble lady,

Si mestes vous procheinne                   still you are near to me in thought

Par penser nuit et jour                          night and day.

Car souvenir me meinne                      For memory so leads me

Si qu'ades sans sejour                           that always without respite

Vo biaute souve reinne,                        your sovereign beauty,

Vo gracieus atour,                                  your gracious appearance,

Vo maniere certeinne                            your sure manner

Et vo fresche coulour                            and fresh color,

Qui n'est pale ne vienne                       neither pale nor rough,

Voy toudis sans sejour                          I see always without respite.

Comment q'ua moy....

 

Alfonso X of Castile, 13th c., A Virgen mut groriosa

A Virgen mut groriosa,                           The glorious Virgin,

Reyanna espirital,                                   spiritual Queen,

dos que ama e ceosa,                              is jealous of those she loves,

ca non quer que facan mal.                 for she does not wish them to do evil.

D'est' un miragre framoso,                 I will tell of a beautiful miracle

ond' averedes sabor,                             in which you will delight;

vos direy, que fez a Virgen                 Holy Mary, Mother of our Lord,

Madre de nostro Sennor,                     performed it and, by so doing,

Per que tirou de gran falla                 freed a young man

a un mui false' amador                         from great sin,

que amyude cambiava                          for he loved many women

seus amores d'un en al.                        and never settled with one.
