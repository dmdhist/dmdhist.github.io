The mausoleum of Theodoric

Before his death in 526, Theodoric built this stone mausoleum for himself, just outside the city walls overlooking the sea.  In the ninth century, it was thought that he had been buried in the vessel that you see below.  Actually, it is an imperial bathtub made of porphyry, a rare reddish-purple stone, but it might have been used by Theodoric for a sarcophagus.
 
 

View of the mausoleum of Theodoric.  The roof is made of a single huge piece of stone.
