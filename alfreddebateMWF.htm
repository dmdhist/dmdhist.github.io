Proposition: The achievements of Alfred were greater than the achievements of Charlemagne



Remember:  you will be called upon to answer at least one of the questions as part of the debate; you may (should) also take part in other parts if you have things to say that differ from what your teammates have said.

Evidence and information


Instead of arguing something that was an issue in the Middle Ages, you are being asked to compare two early medieval rulers.  I leave it up to you how you want to compare them, and on what timescale. You can look at their achievements in the context of their personal life histories (i.e. qualities emphasized by Asser and Einhard); you can look at the lasting impact of their achievements over the next century or two (as I have set up by making this the background information), or you can look at their impact on a longer timescale, down to the present.


The primary source for Charlemagne is Einhard's biography, which you have read for paper 3.  The primary source for Alfred is his biography by Asser, which I have scanned as a PDF file and placed on Canvas under Files, as Asser's Life of Alfred.


The best source of information for the later history of the Carolingian Empire is your textbook, chs. 18 and 21. For the kingdom of England, see Simon Keynes, "England, c. 900-1016", which you can find in Canvas Files as KeynesEngland1999.pdf, and for events through 1042, see M.J. Ryan, "The Age of Aethelred", which you can find in Canvas Files as RyanAgeAethelred2013.pdf. You might also be interested in Richard Abels, "From Alfred to Harold II: the Military Failure of the Late Anglo-Saxon State," which is in Canvas Files as AbelsAlfred2001.pdf.
