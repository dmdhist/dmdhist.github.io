Primary source evidence


Instead of arguing something that was an issue in the Middle Ages, you are being asked to compare two early medieval rulers.  The primary source for Charlemagne, therefore, is Einhard's biography, which you have read for paper 3.  The primary source for Alfred is his biography by Asser, which I have scanned as a PDF file and placed on Canvas under Files, as Asser's Life of Alfred.


There are various ways that arguments could be made, but I think that the one that makes the most sense is to discuss the long-term impact of Charlemagne's and Alfred's achievements.  Naturally you can't take it into the future indefinitely; but you might want to look at the relevant sections in your textbook, on the later Carolingians; we will also have discussed this in class on Nov. 11, and on Alfred.


Another thing that you might want to consider is what qualities or characteristics are stressed by Einhard and Asser.
